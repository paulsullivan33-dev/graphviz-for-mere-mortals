Lab notes - Graphviz for Mere Mortals
======================================

Machine: small Ubuntu 24.04 sandbox.
Tool: Graphviz 2.43.0, distribution packages.
All diagrams invented for the lab. Nothing here describes
a real club, workshop, network or organisation.

What was run
------------
- Five engines (dot, neato, fdp, circo, twopi) report the
  same version line, 2.43.0. Transcript t00.
- first.dot (a fictional committee) renders to SVG with a
  viewBox of 296 by 227 points and 9 <title> elements. t01.
- engines.dot renders under all five engines; every SVG
  names the same 15 <title> elements. Only geometry moves. t02.
- workshop.dot renders to SVG (about 5 KB), PNG (1403 x 181
  pixels, about 39 KB) and PDF (version 1.7). t03.
- broken.dot fails with exit 1 and the message:
  Error: broken.dot: syntax error in line 3 near ';'
  The fault is on line 3 by design: "B -> ;". t04.
- big.dot (40 nodes, three loops, 46 lines) renders under
  dot and under sfdp; both SVGs hold 83 <title> elements. t05.
- pipeline/make_dot.py reads pipeline/steps.csv (5 rows) and
  prints DOT; the render holds 11 <title> elements, including
  the Test -> Sort "fail" loop nobody drew by hand. t06.
- shapes.dot and clusters.dot also render cleanly, 11
  <title> elements each.

Surprises kept in the text
---------------------------
- The recipes chapter originally put a shape attribute on
  an edge statement. It parsed, and would have taught
  nonsense; it was caught in review and rewritten before the
  lab froze its files.
- SVG being text is the lab's whole verification method:
  titles counted, viewBox read, no eyes required.

Not run
-------
- patchwork and osage: characterised from documentation.
- Other operating systems and font sets.
- Graphical editors built on Graphviz by other projects.
